About Me

At iSchool I help run an engaging, student-centred learning environment where coding education is delivered with a blend of technical and pedagogical expertise. My work focuses on crafting interactive sessions, workshops and camps, assessing student progress, and giving tutors and students the constructive feedback that actually moves the needle.

Alongside that, I administer IT infrastructure and lead help desk operations for Holol PMS in the UAE, covering software deployment, licensing, compliance and security protocols. The classroom and the server room use the same muscle: find the problem, fix it clearly, document it so it stays fixed.

When a process is slow, I build the tool that fixes it. That has meant an onboarding system tracking candidates from application to trained tutor, a support platform pairing an Arabic AI tier with human escalation, WhatsApp tooling for tutor support, and training dashboards, mostly TypeScript, React and PostgreSQL, shipped to people who depend on them daily.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Culture and Science City, and I contributed to AI robustness work with Outlier, deliberately generating and analysing mathematical errors to strengthen model fault tolerance and error detection. Earlier roles at Amazon, Teleperformance and Vodafone built the communication and composure I rely on every day.
